On the oracle the same plain-ST chain is impossible from pure USER (first ST traps per finding 2); BK-39's measured oracle self-grant needed the separate PARALLEL_ST primitive.
5. **Oracle-side quirk disclosed, not fully traced:** after the E-K1 trap with KFAULT_PC=0 (which per :1056-1060 should halt), the oracle nonetheless continued stepping and the value landed on a SUPER-mode replay of the same ST. I did not fully identify which arm permitted continuation (vector target was (0,0) with kf=0). This does not affect the divergence verdict — the USER-mode step refused the store on the oracle and never refused on the twin — but the oracle's KFAULT_PC=0 continuation semantics deserve their own look before anyone cites them.
